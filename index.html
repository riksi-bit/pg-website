Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer PG accommodation for both students and working professionals?

Yes — our PG accommodation is open to both students and working professionals.

Is daily housekeeping included?

No, daily housekeeping is not currently offered. Residents are asked to keep the kitchen and house clean and tidy.

Is electricity included in the rent?

No, the electricity bill is charged separately and is not included in the monthly rent.

What is the minimum stay and notice period?

The minimum stay is 2 months, with a 1-month notice period before moving out.
